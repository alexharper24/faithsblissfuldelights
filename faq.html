Common questions

Ordering a custom cake in Bellevue, Ohio

Lead times, pricing, pickup and allergen options: the things people ask before they order. If your question isn't here, just call or text.

How much notice do you need for a custom order?

About a week is preferred. Custom cakes and large cookie trays need the most notice. Rush orders are sometimes possible for an additional fee, so it's always worth asking.

How do I place an order?

Fill in the order form with your occasion, date, flavours and any design ideas. We'll come back to you with availability and a quote before anything is confirmed.

How much does a custom cake cost?

Pricing varies with size, design and detail, so every order is quoted individually. Send through what you have in mind and we'll give you a price. Pricing and pickup are always confirmed before you're locked in.

Do you deliver, or is it pickup only?

Orders are collected by local pickup in the Bellevue, Ohio area. The pickup address is shared once your order is confirmed.

What do you bake?

Custom cakes, hand-decorated cookies, cupcakes, pies, cheesecakes, fresh breads, and treats such as buckeyes and chocolate-dipped strawberries. Everything is made to order. Have a look through the gallery to see what's possible.

Do you offer gluten-free, dairy-free or sugar-free options?

Limited gluten-free, dairy-free and sugar-free options are available. Mention what you need when you order and we'll tell you what's possible.

Can you recreate a cake design I've seen?

Yes. Send a Pinterest picture or point us at a favourite from the gallery and we'll work from that.

Where are you based?

Faith's Blissful Delights is a home bakery in Bellevue, Ohio, with local pickup, an easy drive from Clyde, Castalia, Monroeville, Norwalk, Milan, Sandusky and Fremont.

Still have a question?

Call or text (574) 253-2807, or send the details through the order form and we'll answer everything before you commit.
